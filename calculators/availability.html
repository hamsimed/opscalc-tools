Formula

Inherent availability = MTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR)Achieved availability = Uptime ÷ (Uptime + Downtime)Equivalent downtime per year = (1 − Availability) × 8,760 h

Which availability are you measuring?

  • Inherent availability (from MTBF/MTTR): corrective repair time only. It reflects design and maintainability.
  • Achieved availability: adds preventive maintenance time.
  • Operational availability: includes every delay: waiting for parts, people and permits. It is what production experiences.

Always state which one you report. Production and maintenance teams often argue because they use different definitions.

Availability in series systems

When equipment runs in series with no buffers (such as a chain of conveyors), system availability is roughly the product of individual availabilities. Ten units at 99% each give about 90.4% for the chain. This is why buffers, redundancy and bottleneck focus matter in linked systems.

Moving from 98% to 99% halves the downtime budget. That usually needs a change in maintenance strategy (condition monitoring, redundancy, faster restoration), not just more effort.

Frequently asked questions

Is availability in OEE the same as maintenance availability?

Not exactly. OEE availability measures all unplanned stops against planned production time, including changeovers and material shortages, not only equipment failures.
