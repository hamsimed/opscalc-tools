OEE Calculator

Calculate overall equipment effectiveness from availability, performance, and quality.

Enter your values above.

No information is sent to a server. Calculations run in your browser.

How it works

OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality, with each percentage expressed as a decimal. OEE is a composite manufacturing indicator, not a standalone explanation of losses.

Worked example

A factory line has 90% availability, 85% performance, and 98% quality. Its OEE is 74.97%.

Important limitations

Results depend on your input assumptions. Check your units and validate important business decisions against actual operating data.
