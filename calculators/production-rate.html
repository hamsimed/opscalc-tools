Production Rate Calculator

Calculate average output per hour and compare production to a daily target.

Enter your values above.

No information is sent to a server. Calculations run in your browser.

How it works

Production rate = total good units produced ÷ actual operating hours. For a meaningful comparison, use consistent units and clearly define whether stoppages are included.

Worked example

Producing 900 units in 6 operating hours gives 150 units/hour.

Important limitations

Results depend on your input assumptions. Check your units and validate important business decisions against actual operating data.
