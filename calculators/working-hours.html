Working Hours Calculator

Calculate net working hours after unpaid breaks.

Enter your values above.

No information is sent to a server. Calculations run in your browser.

How it works

Net hours = elapsed shift time − unpaid break. Enter time as a decimal: 9.5 means 09:30. This version supports shifts shorter than 24 hours, including overnight shifts.

Worked example

Starting at 9:00 and finishing at 17:30 with a 30-minute break gives 8 working hours. Enter 17.5 for 17:30.

Important limitations

Results depend on your input assumptions. Check your units and validate important business decisions against actual operating data.
