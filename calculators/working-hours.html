Formula

Decimal hours vs. hours and minutes

Payroll and planning systems usually need decimal hours: 7 h 45 min is 7.75 h, not 7.45. Divide the minutes by 60 to convert. This calculator shows both forms.

Good to know

  • Rules on paid and unpaid breaks, maximum hours and overtime differ by country and by collective agreement. Check the rules that apply to you.
  • For capacity planning, subtract paid breaks and meetings too. That gives the available time used in the takt time calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How does the calculator handle night shifts?

If the end time is earlier than the start time, it assumes the shift ends the next day and adds 24 hours.

How do I total hours for a rotating shift pattern?

Calculate the net hours of each shift type once, multiply by the number of each shift worked in the cycle, and divide by the cycle length in weeks for an average weekly figure. For example, a 4-on/4-off pattern of 12-hour shifts with 1 hour of unpaid break gives 11 h × 4 shifts every 8 days, which averages 38.5 hours per week.
