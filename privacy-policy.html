    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 2026-10-17

    Calculator inputs

    All calculations run locally in your browser. The values you type are not transmitted to us or stored on any server. If you use “Copy link with these inputs”, the values are placed in the link you copy and are visible to anyone you share it with.

    Hosting

    The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical connection data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect the service. See Cloudflare’s own privacy policy for details.

    Analytics

    We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and measure page speed. It does not use cookies, does not store data in your browser and does not identify individual visitors; it records the page visited, the referrer, the browser type, the country and load-time metrics. Calculator values are never sent. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details.

    This site does not use advertising cookies. If we enable Google Analytics 4 in the future, it will run only after you give consent through a banner, with Google signals and ad personalisation disabled, and it will record only which calculators are used. Calculator values will never be sent. This policy will be updated before any such change.

    Advertising

    This site does not currently show advertising. If advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated first to name the provider, describe its cookies and explain your choices, including consent where the law requires it.

    Local storage

    If analytics is enabled, your consent choice will be stored in your browser’s local storage so the banner is not shown again.

    Your rights and contact

    Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not collect calculator data, there is usually nothing to retrieve. For any privacy question, use the contact page.
