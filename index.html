How these tools are built

Each formula is written once in a shared calculation library and checked by automated tests against hand-calculated reference values before publication. Inputs are validated (no silent division by zero or impossible values), units are stated next to every field, and each page explains the limits of the method.

The calculators are written for practitioners: production and maintenance managers, engineers, planners and continuous-improvement teams. They support decisions but do not replace professional judgement or site-specific data.
