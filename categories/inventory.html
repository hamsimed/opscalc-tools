    Inventory & logistics calculators

    Inventory decisions balance service against cost. The reorder point and safety stock calculators decide when to order, EOQ how much, and the carrying cost and turnover calculators what holding stock costs. For bulk material handling, the belt conveyor capacity calculator converts belt geometry and speed into tonnes per hour.

    6 calculators

    • Belt Conveyor Capacity CalculatorCalculate belt conveyor capacity in t/h from load cross-section, belt speed and bulk density, or from load per metre and belt speed.Inventory & Logistics
    • EOQ Calculator (Economic Order Quantity)Calculate the economic order quantity (EOQ), number of orders per year, order interval and total annual ordering and holding cost.Inventory & Logistics
    • Inventory Carrying Cost CalculatorCalculate the annual cost of holding inventory from capital, storage, insurance and obsolescence rates, and get the carrying cost rate for EOQ.Inventory & Logistics
    • Inventory Turnover CalculatorCalculate inventory turnover ratio and days inventory outstanding (DIO) from cost of goods sold and beginning and ending inventory.Inventory & Logistics
    • Reorder Point CalculatorCalculate the reorder point from average daily demand, lead time and safety stock, and check whether your current inventory position means you should order now.Inventory & Logistics
    • Safety Stock CalculatorCalculate safety stock for a target service level from demand variability and lead-time variability, using the combined statistical formula.Inventory & Logistics

    Which calculator do I need?

    • When should I reorder? Reorder point with safety stock.
    • How much should I order? EOQ, using the carrying cost rate.
    • Is stock too high? Turnover and days of inventory.
    • Can the conveyor carry more? Belt conveyor capacity.
