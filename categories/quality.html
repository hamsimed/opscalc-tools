    Quality calculators

    Quality losses cost twice: once in material and once in the capacity used to make or repair a bad part. First pass yield shows how much output is right the first time, and DPMO and sigma level normalise defect rates so different products can be compared.

    2 calculators

    • DPMO & Sigma Level CalculatorCalculate DPMO, defects per unit (DPU), process yield and short-term sigma level from defects, units and opportunities per unit.Quality
    • First Pass Yield & Scrap Rate CalculatorCalculate first pass yield (FPY), scrap rate, rework rate and final yield from units entering a process, units passing first time and units scrapped.Quality

    Which calculator do I need?

    • How much do we rework or scrap? First pass yield.
    • How capable is the process? DPMO and sigma level.
    • What does a defect cost? Combine scrap with the cost per unit calculator.
