    Maintenance & reliability calculators

    Reliability work starts with three numbers: how often equipment fails (MTBF), how long it takes to restore it (MTTR) and the resulting availability. The downtime cost calculator turns stops into money, which is what most improvement decisions need.

    4 calculators

    • Downtime Cost CalculatorEstimate the cost of unplanned downtime from lost output, contribution margin, idle labour and direct repair costs.Maintenance & Reliability
    • Equipment Availability CalculatorCalculate equipment availability from MTBF and MTTR (inherent availability) or from measured uptime and downtime, with equivalent downtime per year.Maintenance & Reliability
    • MTBF CalculatorCalculate mean time between failures (MTBF), failure rate and reliability over a mission time for repairable equipment.Maintenance & Reliability
    • MTTR CalculatorCalculate mean time to repair (MTTR) in hours and minutes, and inherent availability when MTBF is known.Maintenance & Reliability

    Which calculator do I need?

    • Are failures getting more frequent? MTBF over successive periods.
    • Are repairs too slow? MTTR, broken down into response, diagnosis and repair.
    • What availability can we promise? Availability from MTBF and MTTR.
    • Is a spare or an upgrade worth it? Downtime cost, then payback or NPV.
