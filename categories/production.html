    Production & Lean calculators

    These calculators measure how well production time is used and how work flows. Start with OEE to see availability, performance and quality losses in planned time, and TEEP to see capacity left in the calendar. Takt time and cycle time tell you whether a line can keep pace with demand, and Little’s Law links work in process to lead time.

    7 calculators

    • Capacity Utilization CalculatorCalculate capacity utilization (actual vs design capacity) and efficiency (actual vs effective capacity) for a plant, line or machine.Production & Lean
    • Cycle Time CalculatorCalculate average cycle time and throughput from net production time and units completed.Production & Lean
    • Lead Time Calculator (Little’s Law)Estimate process lead time from work in process and throughput using Little’s Law, and calculate process cycle efficiency from value-added time.Production & Lean
    • OEE CalculatorCalculate Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) from shop-floor time and count data or from A, P and Q percentages.Production & Lean
    • Production Rate CalculatorCalculate production rate in units per hour, compare it with a target rate and see the shortfall or surplus in units.Production & Lean
    • Takt Time CalculatorCalculate takt time from shift length, breaks, shifts per day and customer demand, and check whether your bottleneck cycle time can keep up.Production & Lean
    • TEEP CalculatorCalculate TEEP from OEE, planned production time and calendar time, and see how much capacity the schedule leaves unused.Production & Lean

    Which calculator do I need?

    • Is the equipment performing? OEE, then TEEP for the whole calendar.
    • Can we meet demand? Takt time against bottleneck cycle time.
    • Why is lead time long? Little’s Law: lead time grows with WIP.
    • How busy is the plant? Capacity utilization and efficiency.
