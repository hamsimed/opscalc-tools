    Time & workforce calculators

    Time is the base unit of most operations measures. The working hours calculator converts shift times into decimal hours for payroll and planning, and the labor productivity calculator relates output to hours worked.

    2 calculators

    • Labor Productivity CalculatorCalculate labor productivity as output per labor hour, labor hours per unit and output per worker, and compare with a baseline.Time & Workforce
    • Working Hours CalculatorCalculate net working hours from start and end times minus unpaid breaks, including overnight shifts, in hours/minutes and decimal hours.Time & Workforce

    Which calculator do I need?

    • How many hours did a shift last? Working hours, including night shifts.
    • Is the team more productive? Labor productivity against a baseline.
    • How much time is available to produce? Feed net hours into takt time.

    Good practice for time data

    Hours are the denominator of most productivity and cost measures, so small recording errors spread everywhere. Record clock times rather than rounded durations, keep paid and unpaid breaks separate, and state which hours are counted (direct labour only, or all staff). When comparing teams or periods, use the same definition each time and note changes in product mix, because a shift to simpler products raises output per hour without any real improvement.
