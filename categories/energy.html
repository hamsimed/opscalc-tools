    Energy & electrical calculators

    Motors, pumps, fans and compressed air account for a large part of industrial electricity use. These tools estimate energy cost and savings, size electrical loads and convert results into emissions. They follow the logic of an energy review: measure the use, find the significant users, then quantify improvement options.

    Which calculator do I need?

    • What does this machine cost to run? Energy cost.
    • Is energy performance improving? Specific energy consumption.
    • Pumps and fans? Pump power and affinity laws.
    • Compressed air? Leak cost.
    • Electrical checks? kW, kVA, kvar and power factor correction.
    • Emissions? CO₂ from electricity.
