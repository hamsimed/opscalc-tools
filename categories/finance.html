    Business finance calculators

    Operations improvements compete for budget with everything else. These calculators express a project or a product in the terms finance teams use: payback for a first screen, NPV and IRR for value, ROI for comparison, break-even and cost per unit for products.

    5 calculators

    • Break-Even CalculatorCalculate break-even volume and revenue from fixed costs, selling price and variable cost per unit, plus the volume needed for a target profit.Business Finance
    • Cost per Unit CalculatorCalculate the cost per unit from materials, direct labor and overhead for a batch or period, with an optional margin check against selling price.Business Finance
    • NPV & IRR CalculatorCalculate net present value, internal rate of return, profitability index and discounted payback for a project with equal annual cash flows.Business Finance
    • Payback Period CalculatorCalculate simple payback period in years and months from initial investment and annual net cash inflow, and check it against useful life.Business Finance
    • ROI CalculatorCalculate return on investment (ROI), net gain and annualized ROI (CAGR) from investment cost, total return and holding period.Business Finance

    Which calculator do I need?

    • Is the project worth it? NPV at your discount rate, with IRR and discounted payback.
    • How fast does it pay back? Payback period.
    • How many units to cover fixed costs? Break-even.
    • What does one unit cost? Cost per unit.
