About OpsCalc Tools

OpsCalc Tools is an independent collection of free, browser-based calculators for operations, maintenance, energy, inventory and business questions.

The site is built and maintained by an industrial production manager with hands-on experience in operations, maintenance and electrical supervision, continuous improvement and energy management in heavy industry. The calculators reflect the questions that come up daily on the shop floor and in management reviews: how much availability a repair-time reduction buys, what a stop really costs, whether a motor upgrade pays back.

How the calculators are checked

  • Every formula lives in one shared library and is covered by automated tests with hand-calculated reference values.
  • Inputs are validated. Impossible combinations (more good parts than total parts, breaks longer than the shift) are rejected with a clear message.
  • Each page states the formula, units, a worked example and the main limitations of the method.

Limits

Results are estimates based on your inputs. They support planning and learning and do not replace professional engineering, financial or legal advice. See the terms of use.
